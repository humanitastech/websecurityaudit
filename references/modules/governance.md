# ENS/ISO governance

Read [standards.md](../standards.md). Record legal/contractual or voluntary applicability, system boundary, category, dimension levels, risk analysis, responsibilities, supplier obligations, and statement of applicability. Check the actual current requirements and enhancements; module checklists are not a complete ENS catalog.

Create separate records for documentary criteria and technical behavior. Missing policies, approved risk decisions, training records, or audit evidence must remain visible. Distinguish a provisional ISO association from a verified clause. Do not let human acceptance of a finding authorize a compensating control or certify conformity. Record the competent owner and approval evidence where relevant.
